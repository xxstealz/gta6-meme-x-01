# GTA 6 meme for make.fun — gta6-meme-x-01

One GTA 6 meme, drawn from scratch with code (`draw_meme.py`, Python +
PIL): after 13 years of waiting GTA 6 is out and the whole office
called in sick — while make.fun's ghost mascot stands in the Vice City
sunset holding its magenta star coin, next to an OUT OF OFFICE sign.

The entry is the post itself: the meme is posted on X from the public
account @zakrin5c with the make.fun link, @makefunai and the play code
in its text, and `POST.json` names the post. See `DRAFT_POST.md` for
the exact post text and the posting checklist.

- Meme image: `meme.png` (1024x1024 PNG, first picture of the post)
- Draw script: `draw_meme.py` (deterministic, no network)
- Entry program: `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only — copies `POST.json`
  to `/output`)
- Post file: `POST.json` (placeholder until the user posts and sends
  back the post URL)
